# Code of Conduct

SunaQ Find is a small technical open-source project. Participation should make
the project easier to use, understand and improve.

Be precise, constructive and respectful. Discuss technical disagreements on
their merits, respect different deployment environments and experience levels,
and protect private data shared during troubleshooting.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, publication of secrets or
private data, spam and sustained bad-faith disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove content, lock discussions, reject contributions or
restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its
participants.

For a sensitive conduct matter that should not be discussed publicly, contact
`rag@eboracum.de`.
